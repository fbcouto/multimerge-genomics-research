# Your own problems

Put the notebooks for your own questions here. Create one from the template with

```
python notebooks/new_problem.py my_problems/<name> "Title of the problem"
```

which fills in the title and never overwrites an existing notebook. Keep the template's sections (the problem,
data, steps with their reasons, checks, methods paragraph), put your inputs at the top of the data cell, and
another researcher can rerun and adapt your analysis. Subfolders of your own (`my_problems/<topic>/<name>`) work too.
